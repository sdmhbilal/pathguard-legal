Privacy Policy

PathGuard · Last updated 24 September 2026

Who we are

PathGuard is a personal-safety companion. You choose trusted contacts, start a walk or enable Auto-watch, and those contacts can see that you are on your way. It is not an emergency dispatch service and it does not sell your location.

Age

PathGuard is for people 13 and older. We collect a date of birth at signup so we can refuse accounts under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have, contact us and we will delete the account.

Information we collect

Account: name, email, password hash, phone number, date of birth, optional emergency notes, and an optional photo URL.

Device: push tokens so we can send SOS, check-in, and Auto-watch notifications. We do not use an advertising SDK.

Location: precise GPS points and geofence events for walks and Auto-watch journeys, plus the saved places you pin (home, work, school, or custom) and the fence radius you set.

Journey history: route points, arrival, check-ins, SOS events, and Auto-watch pins so you and the contacts on that journey can review what happened.

How location is used

You opt in. Location is shared only during an active walk or an in-transit Auto-watch journey. There is no 24/7 live map while you are at a saved place.

Walks: after you start a SafeWalk, the app uploads GPS so chosen contacts can see your route until you arrive, end, or cancel.

Auto-watch: you enable it on the phone that is tracked. Geofences stay registered around your saved places while it is on so the phone can notice when you leave. Continuous GPS runs only after you leave a place, with a visible indicator, and stops when you arrive, cancel, or the journey expires.

On Android, Auto-watch needs Always / all-the-time location. The app may also ask you to allow unrestricted battery use and, on some phones, auto-start or background activity in system settings so leave and arrive can still be detected when the screen is locked. Those prompts do not collect extra personal information; they only change how the operating system treats the app.

Force-stopping the app, turning location off, or denying Always location stops continuous GPS. Geofences may still wake the app if Auto-watch remains enabled and the operating system allows it. Some Android battery savers delay or block those wakes.

Who can see your location

Only trusted contacts you invited (or accepted) and linked to that walk or Auto-watch journey. Contacts must accept before they can watch. You can remove a contact. We do not share your pin with the public, advertisers, or data brokers.

Retention and deletion

Account data, saved places, walk history, and Auto-watch history stay with your account until you delete it in Settings. Deleting the account ends open journeys, revokes sessions and devices, and removes personal data we store. We do not currently offer a shorter auto-delete window for location points.

Service providers

We use hosting, email delivery, push (APNs / FCM), and mapping providers to run the product. They process data only to provide those services. We do not sell personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes in a material way, we will update the date above and post the new version at this URL.

Contact

Privacy questions: use in-app Settings or the contact email listed with the PathGuard store listing. You can also delete your account in Settings at any time.
